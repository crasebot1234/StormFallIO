Settings Customize your Stormfall experience

Changes are saved automatically in this browser. Graphics changes apply immediately; gameplay options take effect in your next match where noted.

Graphics

Auto adjusts quality based on performance. Render scale trades image sharpness for frame rate. Higher presets use more GPU and memory.

Aim & Camera

Aim assist slows your look near opponents; High also adds bullet magnetism. Aim assist is disabled while building, driving, or using a scope.

Audio

Music: “Exhilarate”, “Volatile Reaction”, “Sneaky Adventure”, and “Hitman” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0.

Gameplay

Bot difficulty and time of day are chosen when a match starts. Voice chat uses your browser’s speech features when supported.

Left stick
Move. Push it all the way forward to sprint
Drag right side
Look around and aim
FIRE · AIM
Hold FIRE to shoot or swing · AIM toggles aiming down sights (in build mode it swaps material)
JUMP · CROUCH
Jump, dive from the plane, open the glider, handbrake in cars · CROUCH toggles crouching
USE
Glows when you can open a chest, pick something up, open a door or get in a car
RELOAD · BUILD
Reload your gun · switch to build mode, then tap WALL, FLOOR, RAMP or ROOF and FIRE to place
Hotbar
Tap a slot to switch weapons or items
Top buttons
ITEMS inventory · DROP selected item · SHOP · EMOTE · CAM camera view · MAP · II pause
Driving
The stick steers and drives · USE gets out
W A S D
Move · Shift sprint · Ctrl crouch · Space jump · Ctrl while sprinting to slide
Mouse
Left fire / swing sword · Right aim down sights · Wheel cycles slots · Middle click pings
1–6 · F
Inventory slots · Storm Blade (sword)
E · R
Interact (chests, pickups, doors, cars, vending machines) · reload (every reload takes 2 s or less)
H
Drop the selected hotbar item
Enter
Chat. With Voice chat on, just speak after pressing Enter, then press Enter again to send
Ammo
Each gun uses one ammo type. Walk over ammo boxes to collect. Every eliminated player drops ammo for their guns
Tab · U
Inventory · gold shop (buy ammo, heals, weapons and upgrades)
Q / B · Z X C V
Build mode · wall, floor, ramp, roof · G edits a wall
P · L
Cycle camera angle · swap shoulder
T · Enter
Emote · chat (bots may answer)
Vehicles
W/S throttle · A/D steer · Space handbrake · Shift boost · H horn
Space
Jump from the Battle Bus · open glider · W while skydiving to dive
M · Esc
Full map · pause menu
